TraceSynth: Generating Production-Quality Kernel Traces with Constraint-Guided Diffusion Models

Yuvraj Sehgal | Sneh Patel | Mahsa Panahandeh | Naser Ezzati-Jivan | Francois Tetreault

kernel-tracing system-tracing trace-analysis machine-learning

Core contribution: TraceSynth studies constraint-guided diffusion models for generating realistic kernel traces.

Problem and motivation

Production kernel traces can be difficult to share or obtain at scale, limiting experimentation and evaluation of trace-analysis methods.

Method and contribution

The paper combines diffusion-based generation with constraints intended to preserve properties of production-quality kernel traces.

Findings and evidence

The work proposes a generative approach for kernel-trace synthesis; fidelity measures, constraints, and evaluation results require full-text review.

Limitations and future directions

Limitations: The current catalog captures bibliographic evidence but not the full text; quantitative results, implementation details, and paper-specific validity threats require source review.

Future work: Compare synthetic and real traces across kernels, workloads, event distributions, and downstream analysis tasks.
