Catalog abstract summary

The accessible Springer preview associates the paper with a unified anomaly-detection approach for bandwidth-, application-, and connection-exhaustion DDoS groups, using CUSUM-style change detection and representative HTTP, database/application, and TCP SYN-flood attacks.

Source: Springer two-page preview, paraphrased; complete article not obtained.

Problem and motivation

Cloud DDoS attacks can consume different resource layers and require detection methods that distinguish their behavioral signatures.

Method and contribution

The two-page preview describes one inclusive detector that compares normal and potential-attack traffic/resource behavior across bandwidth, application, and connection exhaustion, with CUSUM for change detection. It mentions representative HTTP, database/application, and TCP SYN-flood classes, but does not expose the complete toolchain, feature set, dataset protocol, or baselines.

Findings and evidence

The preview supports the attack-taxonomy and unified-detection framing, but exposes no sample counts, train/test protocol, hardware, detection rate, false-positive value, or other quantitative result.

Limitations and future directions
